Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Coach Syd.

What happens after I submit my application? expand_more

Syd will review your details personally to ensure online coaching is the right fit for your goals. You will receive an email within 24-48 hours with next steps and onboarding details.

Do I need a gym membership or can I workout at home? expand_more

Every plan is 100% customized to your equipment setup. Whether you have a full commercial gym, a few dumbbells at home, or resistance bands, your routine will be built to optimize your results.

How does the weekly check-in process work? expand_more

Every week you submit your progress photos, measurements, lifting numbers, and habit logs inside the coaching app. Syd sends back an in-depth video breakdown with adjustments and coaching feedback.

Will I have to give up my favorite foods? expand_more

No! We use flexible dieting principles. You will learn how to hit your protein and macro targets while still enjoying social meals, vacations, and treats in moderation.
